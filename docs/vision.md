# Unloop — Vision (3–5 years)

> *"An open architecture to free human attention and reconnect people to the real world."*

---

## 1. The future of the Unloop ecosystem

Unloop’s MVP starts on mobile (Android and iOS) with local interruption of doomscrolling in short video. Digital overstimulation and autopilot show up across many screens and contexts.

Over the next 3–5 years, Unloop aims to grow from a mobile app into a **coordinated, multi-platform ecosystem** for digital wellbeing and mental health.

---

## 2. Long-term pillars

### A. Encrypted Buddy Network (P2P social accountability)
* **Friend as ally:** Mutual support without judgment or punishment.
* **Unpredictable interventions:** A Buddy may send audio, a funny photo, a meme, a shared memory, or a voice note that fires when a threshold is crossed—breaking visual trance with a real bond.
* **End-to-end encryption and zero server store:** Peer-oriented messaging via a stateless relay—no profiles, no third-party cloud retention, privacy first.

### B. Empathic, contextual AI Companion
* **Neutral mirror:** A local / privacy-preserving AI interlocutor when a human Buddy is unavailable or unwanted.
* **Active reflection questions:** The AI does not merely block; it asks calibrated questions (e.g. *“Are you done with your break, or are you avoiding that email?”*) in a tone the user chose (Wise, Ironic, Pragmatic).
* **Behavioral adaptation:** Recognize fatigue patterns and propose micro-challenges suited to the moment.

### C. Multi-screen and multi-context expansion
* **Browser extension (Web Unloop):** The same interruption engine on desktop browsers for YouTube Web, X/Twitter, and news feeds.
* **Desktop apps (macOS / Windows / Linux):** Context monitoring for work or study sessions.
* **Wearables and IoT (Apple Watch, Wear OS):** Subtle haptics or physical challenges on the wrist.

### D. Open Challenge ecosystem and community catalog
* **Open framework:** Developers, psychologists, and educators create extensible Challenge categories via standard APIs.
* **Open-source shared catalog:** A public directory (curated for privacy) to explore, vote on, and install challenge packs.

---

## 3. Social, scientific, and educational impact

### A. Schools and parent associations
* **Awareness kits:** Free, safe tools educators can recommend in talks with students and families.
* **Non-punitive family use:** Turn phone management from conflict into a shared, game-like responsibility.

### B. Scientific research and data privacy
* **Opt-in anonymous / local research protocols:** Ethical voluntary sharing of fully anonymized aggregates with universities studying digital addiction and mental health.
* **Open APIs for digital wellbeing:** Aim to become a de-facto open-source standard for ethical tools.

---

## 4. What Unloop will never become

Hard red lines:

* **Never a social network:** No feeds, likes, followers, or vanity metrics that create more screen dependency.
* **Never monetization of health:** Attention-protection features will never be paywalled as Basic vs Premium.
* **Never selling data:** No ad aggregators or data brokers get access to usage behavior.
