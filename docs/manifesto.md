# Unloop Manifesto

> *"We did not lose our willpower. Technology was redesigned to overwhelm our biological defenses."*

---

## 1. The problem: autopilot trance

The smartphone itself is not the enemy. Nobody opens Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube intending to waste two hours on content they will forget five minutes later.

The real problem is **autopilot mode** (*doomscrolling*): a motor and cognitive trance induced by algorithms tuned to the millisecond to remove natural stopping points. Variable-interval reinforcement (the swipe as a slot machine) plus prefrontal fatigue shut down our rational ability to choose when to stop.

In that moment, the lucid user disappears. Only a mechanical gesture remains.

---

## 2. Our philosophy: restore awareness, do not punish

Most digital-wellbeing apps fail because they act like a jailer: hard blocks, punitive timers, and guilt. Result? Users get angry, disable permissions, or uninstall.

**Unloop rejects the punitive model.**

* **We are not a lock:** we are a pause.
* **We are not a prison:** we are a mirror.
* **We do not judge:** we interrupt the pattern.

Unloop’s goal is not to stop you from using your phone, but to **break the trance for 5–15 seconds** with an action incompatible with scrolling. We want to reactivate the rational mind and ask a simple question: *“Do you really want to continue, or did you ask me to stop you?”*

Unloop is the bridge between the **lucid** user (who configured the app yesterday) and the user in **autopilot** (who is scrolling today).

---

## 3. Foundational ethical principles

To be an unconditionally trustworthy tool, Unloop rests on four non-negotiable pillars:

### 1. Zero-data and total privacy
Your usage data, habits, and contacts must never leave your phone. We do not track what you watch, we do not store statistics on external servers, and we do not build behavioral profiles. The system operates entirely on-device.

### 2. 100% open source
The code is public, transparent, and verifiable by anyone. There are no hidden mechanisms, backdoors, or opaque algorithms. The project belongs to the community.

### 3. No ads, no premium “lock-in”
Unloop is non-profit. There will be no ad banners, and no essential digital-health features locked behind aggressive subscriptions. Donations cover only living costs (for example the Apple Developer license).

### 4. Designed to disappear
The most successful app is the one you no longer need. Unloop does not want to capture your attention or keep you inside it: its only purpose is to return you to real life as quickly as possible.

---

## 4. The experience: human, empathic, and lightly ironic

Breaking a compulsive habit requires empathy, not austerity.

Unloop uses irony, creativity, physical interaction, and complicity (friends or challenge partners) to turn the interruption from frustration into a brief moment of awareness.

We do not say *“Time’s up.”* We communicate: *“I’m interrupting you because you asked me to.”*

If a challenge times out without success, we do not punish or keep the feed locked. We release the interrupt and leave a brief, non-moralizing wake-up line — awareness, not a ban.
