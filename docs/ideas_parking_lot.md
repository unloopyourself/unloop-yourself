# Unloop — Ideas parking lot

> *"Extraordinary ideas that will NOT enter the MVP so we can ship something focused and real."*

---

## Parking rule

When a fascinating idea risks derailing MVP focus, record:

1. **Name**
2. **Why it is interesting**
3. **Why it is not in the MVP**

The idea is preserved, not lost—frozen for later.

---

## 1. Hardware, devices, platforms

### Smartwatch integration (Apple Watch and Wear OS)
* **Interesting:** Subtle wrist haptics or completing a challenge on-watch (steps, heart rate).
* **Not MVP:** Extra companion apps and test matrices.

### Desktop browser extension
* **Interesting:** Same engine against YouTube Web / social feeds at work.
* **Not MVP:** Validate first on the primary compulsive device—the phone.

### Home-screen widget
* **Interesting:** Autopilot Score or cooldown at a glance.
* **Not MVP:** Peripheral UI; does not prove the interrupt hypothesis.

---

## 2. AI and media

### Meme generator / customized shocked-face shield
* **Interesting:** Humor breaks motor patterns.
* **Not MVP:** Camera/library/graphics complexity; static or simple copy is enough.

### Realtime voice / avatar AI Companion
* **Interesting:** Spoken reflection during the challenge.
* **Not MVP:** API cost, network dependence, latency.

### On-device local LLM
* **Interesting:** Fully offline Companion.
* **Not MVP:** Size and battery cost on modest phones.

---

## 3. Social accountability and gamification

*(Additional parked social/gamification ideas may be appended here as they arise. Keep MVP free of vanity metrics and social-network dynamics—see manifesto red lines.)*

### Autopilot Score as social leaderboard
* **Interesting:** Motivation via comparison.
* **Not MVP:** Risks vanity metrics and competitive shame; conflicts with “designed to disappear.”

### Public challenge marketplace in v1
* **Interesting:** Community growth.
* **Not MVP:** Security, curation, and scope explosion before core interrupt is validated.

### Challenge feedback (heart or thumbs)
* **Interesting:** After a challenge, a single **♥** or **👍/👎** (local only) can down-weight hated interrupts and gently prefer ones that feel fair—without a ratings feed.
* **Not MVP:** Needs the multi-challenge pack + on-device weight store first; easy to misuse as gamification if we show counts or streaks.

### Challenge traces and local stats
* **Interesting:** On-device counts (shown / completed / timed out per challenge), optional short history — helps the user tune Settings → Challenges and proves the interrupt worked.
* **Not MVP:** Easy to become a second dashboard habit. If shipped later: local-only, no social, no streaks-as-score, bury under Settings, never a home-screen vanity chart. Prefer “enough to decide” over engagement metrics.
