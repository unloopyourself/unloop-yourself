Unloop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25
Contact: unloopyourself.dev@gmail.com
Package: io.github.unloopyourself.unloop

Unloop is a privacy-first, open-source Android app that briefly interrupts doomscrolling with a short on-device challenge. This page explains what the app does with data.

Summary

  • No account required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash SDKs that send behavioral data off-device.
  • Usage monitoring and challenge progress stay on your device.
  • You can revoke permissions or uninstall at any time.

On-device processing

ProcessingWhy
Usage Access (UsageStats)Detect watched apps in the foreground and accumulate time toward your threshold
Display over other appsShow the interrupt / challenge UI over the feed
Foreground service + notificationKeep monitoring reliable while you use other apps
Sensors (accelerometer / gyroscope)Optional physical challenges you enable
Local settings & audit logRemember feeds, timings, enabled challenges on device

Unloop’s product intent is not to build a cloud profile of what you watch.

What we do not collect

By default Unloop does not create accounts, upload usage history or challenge results, sell data to advertisers, or embed third-party behavioral telemetry SDKs.

Permissions

Permissions exist so the interrupt loop can work as you configured it. Deny or revoke them in Android Settings; monitoring will not run until required permissions are granted again.

Children

Unloop is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it under the applicable age for digital wellbeing tools in your region without a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The date above will change. Material changes will appear in the open-source repository and on this page.
